What Is a Sunflower? A Kid-Friendly Guide

August 6, 2026

TL;DR

A sunflower is a tall, fast-growing plant with a big round flower made of yellow petals and a dark center full of seeds. It's one of the easiest first plants for a young child to grow because it sprouts quickly and gets visibly taller every few days.

If your child asks "what is a sunflower?", here's the simple answer: it's a plant that starts as a tiny seed, grows into a tall green stalk, and ends with one giant flower on top that looks a little like a smiling sun.

What does a sunflower look like?

A sunflower has a thick, hairy green stem, big rough leaves shaped like hearts, and one large flower head at the top. The flower has bright yellow petals around the edge and a dark brown or black center packed with hundreds of tiny seeds.

Why does it follow the sun?

Young sunflowers actually turn during the day to face the sun as it moves across the sky — this is a real, scientifically documented behavior kids find genuinely magical to watch. Point it out each morning and evening and let your child notice the difference themselves.

How does a sunflower grow?

Plant the seed about an inch deep in soil, water it, and give it plenty of sunlight. Within a week or two, a tiny green sprout pushes up. Over the next couple of months it grows taller and taller, and eventually a flower bud appears at the top and opens up.

Fun facts kids love

  • A sunflower's "petals" around the edge are actually made of many tiny flowers, and the dark center is made of hundreds more.
  • Sunflower seeds are edible — the same seeds people snack on come from these plants.
  • Some giant sunflowers can grow taller than an adult in a single summer.

Frequently asked questions

Why do sunflowers turn to face the sun?
Young sunflowers move to follow the sun across the sky, a behavior called heliotropism, because it helps the plant soak up more sunlight while it's still growing. Once the flower is fully mature, it usually stops moving and settles facing east.
How tall can a sunflower grow?
Most sunflower varieties grown at home reach 3 to 6 feet tall, though some giant varieties can grow well over 10 feet in a single season.
How long does it take a sunflower to bloom?
Sunflowers typically sprout within 7 to 10 days and bloom their first flower about 70 to 90 days after planting, depending on the variety.
