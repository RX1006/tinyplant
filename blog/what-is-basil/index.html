What Is Basil? A Kid-Friendly Guide

August 9, 2026

TL;DR

Basil is a fast-growing herb with bright green leaves that smell strong and sweet the moment you touch them. It's a favorite first plant for kids because it engages their sense of smell, not just sight — rubbing a single leaf is enough to notice something is happening.

If your child asks "what is basil?", here's the simple answer: it's a plant you grow for its leaves, not a flower or a vegetable, and the leaves smell amazing the second you touch them.

What does basil look like?

Basil grows as a bushy little plant with bright green, oval-shaped leaves and a square-ish stem, which is unusual and fun for kids to notice by touch. As it grows, it produces more and more leaves, and eventually small white or purple flower spikes if left to grow long enough.

Why does it smell so strong?

Basil leaves are full of natural oils, and those oils are what create that unmistakable sweet, slightly spicy smell. You don't need to crush the leaf hard — a gentle rub between two fingers is enough to release the scent, which is a great sensory moment to share with a young child.

How does basil grow?

Plant seeds shallowly in soil, keep them warm and consistently moist, and give the plant plenty of sunlight. Sprouts appear within a week or two, and the plant fills out with pickable leaves within about a month.

Fun facts kids love

  • Picking basil leaves actually helps the plant grow bushier instead of hurting it.
  • Basil is used in cooking all over the world, especially in Italian and Southeast Asian dishes.
  • The stem of a basil plant is square, not round — something kids can actually feel for themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Why does basil smell so strong?
Basil leaves contain natural oils that release a strong, sweet-spicy scent whenever the leaf is touched, crushed, or brushed against — which is why just rubbing a leaf gently between your fingers releases its smell instantly.
Can kids pick basil leaves without hurting the plant?
Yes. Picking a few outer leaves doesn't harm a healthy basil plant — in fact, regularly picking leaves encourages the plant to grow bushier, so it's a safe, repeatable activity for kids.
How long does basil take to grow?
Basil typically sprouts within 5 to 10 days and has enough leaves to start picking within about 3 to 4 weeks of planting.
