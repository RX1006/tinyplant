What Is a Snap Pea? A Kid-Friendly Guide

August 7, 2026

TL;DR

A snap pea is a climbing plant that grows curly little arms called tendrils to pull itself up a pole or fence, and produces crunchy green pods that kids can eat whole — pod and all. It's a favorite first plant because the climbing is fun to watch and the payoff is a snack you grew yourself.

If your child asks "what is a snap pea?", here's the simple answer: it's a vegetable plant that climbs upward using little curly threads, and grows pods you can eat straight off the vine, no shelling required.

What does a snap pea look like?

Snap peas grow as a vine with small oval leaves and delicate white or purple flowers. From the flowers, flat green pods appear, which plump up as the peas inside grow. Curling tendrils — thin, springy little threads — reach out from the vine looking for something to grab onto.

Why does it climb?

Snap peas are natural climbers. Give them a pole, trellis, or netting, and their tendrils will curl around it and pull the vine upward. Watching a tendril find and wrap around a support over a day or two is one of the most satisfying things for a young gardener to check on.

How does a snap pea grow?

Plant the seed directly in soil about an inch deep, give it something to climb nearby, and keep the soil moist. A sprout appears within one to two weeks, and the vine climbs steadily upward over the following weeks until flowers and then pods appear.

Fun facts kids love

  • Unlike regular peas, you eat the whole pod on a snap pea — no shelling needed.
  • The curly tendrils move and grab onto things in real time if you watch closely over a few hours.
  • Snap peas grow best in cooler weather, so they're often one of the first things planted in spring.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat the whole snap pea pod?
Yes. Unlike regular garden peas, snap peas have a tender, edible pod, so kids can eat the entire thing — pod and peas together — straight off the vine.
Why do snap peas need something to climb?
Snap peas grow curling tendrils that reach out and grab onto poles, netting, or fences, using them as support so the vine can climb upward toward more sunlight instead of sprawling on the ground.
How long do snap peas take to grow?
Snap peas usually sprout within 7 to 14 days and are ready to harvest about 60 to 70 days after planting.
